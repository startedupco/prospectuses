Candidates must meet the individual subject admission requirements for the qualification. The Candidate must pass the theoretical and practical interviews set by Campuses offering the programme.
Objectives
To develop occupational competence by equipping learners with integrated knowledge, practical
skills, and workplace experience relevant to specific occupations.
To align education and training with labour market needs through industry-driven qualification
design and implementation.
To enhance employability and career progression by providing nationally recognised and portable
qualifications.
To promote standardisation and quality assurance in the development, delivery, and assessment of
occupational qualifications.
To contribute to national skills development priorities by addressing scarce and critical skills in key
economic sectors.
To strengthen collaboration between education, training providers, and employers in the design and
delivery of workplace-based learning.
