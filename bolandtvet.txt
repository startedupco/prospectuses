OCCUPATIONAL CERTIFICATE
Bookkeeper (Level 5)











What you should know

The purpose of this qualification is to prepare the student to operate as a Financial Administration Clerk by assisting in the processes and procedures of fundamental financial aspects of a business or institution through financial recordkeeping and providing support for effective financial operations. This includes recording transactions, maintaining financial records up to Trail Balance, reconciliations and maintaining effective customer relationships.


Requirements
Grade 12 Certificate / NCV Level 4 / Relevant FETC at NQF Level 4


Duration & Credits
• 3 Years
• Number of credits: 364


Career Opportunities
• Accounting Clerk
• Financial Administrator
• Accounting Administrator
• Credit Controller
• Financial Administration Clerk
• Payroll Administrator
• Debtors Clerk
• Creditors Clerk
• Senior Bookkeeper

Use accounting systems to record accounting transactions
Calculate, compile and submit relevant VAT submissions
Apply knowledge of booking principles and practices
Use accounting packages
Use payroll software packages to record salaries and wages and submit SARS returns
Manage petty cash and stock/inventory control
Prepare and make payments
Process transfers
Prepare Cash Flow Statements
Prepare Income Statements
Prepare other relevant statements

NATIONAL CERTIFICATE (VOCATIONAL) (L2–4)
Safety in Society




What you should know

This programme consists of four vocational subjects namely Policing Practices, South African Criminal Law, Governance and the Criminal Justice System. Each student will be taught on a theoretical basis what role each subject fulfils in terms of who and where acts are formulated, who imparts and enforces these roles in communities, what the consequences of violating the law is and how different departments function to maintain the process within a democratic system.


Requirements
