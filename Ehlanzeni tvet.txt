Civil Engineering and Building Construction
Designed to equip students with practical skills and real-world knowledge for a successful career journey.

Programme type
NC(V) Programme
Levels
NQF Levels 2-4
Duration
One year per level
The NC(V) in Civil Engineering and Building Construction develops practical construction skills alongside the theory needed to work on modern building sites, from planning and materials through to masonry or plumbing specialisations.

What you will study
Construction Planning
Materials
Plant and Equipment
Masonry or Plumbing
Compulsory fundamentals (including English and Mathematics)
Admission requirements
Level 2: Grade 9 year-end results with Mathematics passed, or an NQF Level 1 qualification, plus the placement assessment
Level 3: NQF Level 2 in the same programme
Level 4: NQF Level 3 in the same programme
Where it can take you
Architectural engineering
Quantity surveying
Building construction
Industrial design
Town and regional planning
Sanitation engineering
Plumbing
Carpentry
