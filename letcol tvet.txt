BUSINESS MANAGEMENT
Admission Requirements
Grade 12 certificate in the field of study with an APS Score of 24
NCV Level 4 in the relevant field as per Admission policy of the College
N4 Introductory Certificate in the relevant field
Outcomes
The students will be trained in Communication, Office Administration, Computer Skills, sales Management, Public Relations, Accounting and Entrepreneurship to enable him/her to work in a business environment
Career Opportunities
Financial Clerk
Accounting Clerk
Credit Clerk
Private and Public Accounting
Financial Services
Bookkeeping
Banking
Self-employment
Duration
A minimum of 18 months (semester course)
Curriculum
Students in posession of N6 Certificate plus 18 months (2000 hours) relevant experience can apply for a National D-Diploma (NQF L6) in Business Management
