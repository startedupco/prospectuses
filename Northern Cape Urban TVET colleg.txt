Admission Requirements
Minimum entry requirements, courses offered and how to apply online for Northern Cape Urban TVET College programmes 2024.

 

After knowing that your program of study is among the List of Courses offered at Northern Cape Urban TVET College, It’s advisable to check out the basic the admission entry requirements for that course.

NATIONAL VOCATION CERTIFICATE NC(V)
To be accepted for the following NC(V) Programs you must have:
 
The minimum requirements for all NCV is Grade 9.

 For Finance, Economics and Accounting you must have passed Accounting as a subject

  

NATIONAL DIPLOMA

To be accepted for the following NATED programs you must have:

Your AP Score will be applied upon registration.

 

National Senior Certificate

NQF Level 4

 

For Financial Management you must have National Senior Certificate with Accounting

 


LEARNERSHIP

 

The Business Unit organises various accredited and non-accredited part-time and full-time courses on demand such as:

 

Hairdressing: NQF Level 2-4  (Duration 1 year per level)

 

Early Childhood Development: ECD Level 4 – 5 (Duration 1year per level)

 

Business Administration: Level 4 (Duration 1 year per level)

 

Association of Accounting Technicians (AAT)SA: Level 4 (Duration 1 year per level)

Northern Cape Urban TVET College Online Application Form 2024
The Northern Cape Urban TVET College 2024 applications is open for NCV, NATED, Diploma, Certificate and other programmes offered at the college.



N/B: During application, An inaccurate or incomplete application form will hold up the processing of your application. Please note that if you provide any false information your application will be rejected immediately.

If you want your application to be processed faster;



Make Sure:

You must have a valid email address. Please create an email address, if you don’t have one. We will correspond and reply to your email address during the application process.

During the application process you will be requested to upload copies of your documents like:

• A certified copy of your ID

• Proof of Residence etc.



Foreign students:

• Certified copies of passport

• SAQA approved foreign qualification

• Proof of medical insurance or cover

• Valid study permit
